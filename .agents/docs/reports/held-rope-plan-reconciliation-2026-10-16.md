Three source-level implications matter most. First, a local semantic edit can still copy clean packed gaps and visit post-prefix topology: rope sharing alone is not an end-to-end bound. Second, the new ordered-range call removes repeated rope traversals for scattered dirty physical slots, but its dense one-interval case has no promised win and it cannot cure dense 512/1,024 regressions by itself.
