{/*
reader
- Browsing. Wants to see what the library does before reading anything; will click through to the page behind the
  scene that catches them.

write · intro
- Question: what am I looking at?
- Say: one card per example, a still of the scene, in a grid; rest on a card and it runs, two at a time on
  one renderer; every caption is the page that explains it. Say nothing else — the pictures are the page.
- Shape: two sentences above the grid, none below.

notes · internals for the page
- the cards are `<glyph-proxy>` windows under one pointer-activated root (`site/docs/components/explainer/root.tsx`,
  `activation="pointer"`, `hover-delay`); stills come from `poster`, captured from the scenes themselves into
  `site/examples/public/thumbnails/<slug>.webp`
- the same scenes, one at a time, live at `/examples/?example=<slug>`; the masonry gallery at `/examples/`
- every slug is a `site/examples/src/catalog.ts` entry; a scene the catalog lacks shows a planned card
*/}
