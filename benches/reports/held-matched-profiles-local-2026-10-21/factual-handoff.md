All sampled self values are totals across each profile, while p50/p95 are callback wall durations from the profile run. The phase anchors are fresh disjoint child nodes under `updateBulk`, validated by `benchmark:cpu-profile-summary`; their sampled durations plus detached samples partition each whole profile. Sampled CPU divided by 80 gives only sampled CPU per callback, not a wall-time coefficient. Inclusive ancestry values must not be added.

The phase call trees show main and published place most shaping and other Wasm work under publication; E2–E6 and V3 place shaping and other Wasm work under setter/preparation. Within V3, the setter/preparation subtree has 479.9 ms shaping and 975.9 ms other-Wasm sampled self, versus E4 244.2 and 456.8 ms. V3's publication subtree has 200.0 ms gather and 72.7 ms render-plan sampled self versus E4 104.0 and 37.6 ms. This locates observed work; it does not prove why V3's one-shot durations rose.

For direct named-function inspection, V3 setter/preparation includes `validate_planner_inputs` 96.2 ms self (E4 42.5), `prepare_flow_layout` 64.7 (E4 13.8), `append_run_local_cluster` 61.5 (E4 13.5), and HarfRust `apply_forward` 76.0 (E4 62.4). V3 publication includes `commit_all` 98.9 (E4 7.0), `append_retained_stream_in_range` 77.9 (E4 symbol absent), `CodecGatherWorkspace::update_fields` 61.1 (E4 symbol absent), `prepare_internal` 49.5 (E4 13.2), and `prepare_placement_slots` 46.4 (E4 18.3). Differences in symbol presence may reflect changed optimization or call structure; they are not allocation counts. Full original function names, archive-backed source mapping, phase stage totals, self sample counts, and raw Wasm URLs are preserved in each `cpu-summary.md` and `comparison.json`.

The V3 profile's `timeDeltas` have p99 8.0 ms and one 66.8 ms gap; main and E4 p99 were 1.54 and 2.33 ms. The V3 callback p95 of 148.5 ms is a noisy one-shot observation. A process snapshot **after** all captures found a busy Git process and Spotlight workers, but it does not establish their state during any capture; see `process-context-after-captures.json` and capture times in `capture-windows-and-deltas.json`. No arm was resampled. The normal profile workflow authenticates the installed shaper Wasm but does not independently hash its full installed package tree. These profiles do not replace the unprofiled seven-family acceptance matrix; that matrix was not run.
