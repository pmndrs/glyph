## Important clean findings

- `retained_rope.rs::update_ordered_node` reserves a replacement vector once at first Replace, bounded by the existing leaf length (at most 32). Its length tracks the next old ordinal to emit. It copies the untouched prefix, gaps, Keep rows, and suffix, including the stopped row; Replace does not copy the replaced old row. No Replace returns `None` for that leaf and keeps its Arc. The updater assigns its root only after recursion and range validation succeed, so callback/storage error preserves the prior root. Generic Stop still adopts the successful prefix. The flat-record and shared-root tests at `ordered_ranges_copy_each_leaf_once_across_owner_boundaries`, `ordered_stop_preserves_prefix_and_copies_only_unchanged_old_rows`, and `ordered_range_stream_failure_and_stop_preserve_their_contracts` check distinct aspects of this contract. I found no off-by-one in the lazy fill or Stop path.
- `ordered_plan.rs::retained_physical_index` and `compare_retained_instance` share mapping/key/slot admission and dirt logic between complete and sparse scopes. The complete `update_ordered` callback derives and compares each admitted row once, then stages replacements in a local rope. A late key/mapping miss returns private `RetainedAdmissionError::Miss`; arithmetic, reserve, and state errors remain `Plan` errors, and rope storage errors remain `AllocationFailed`. `prepare_internal` resets the existing pending dirt before the full fallback. `prepare_retained_topology` assigns `self.pending_instances` only after the entire admission and pending-batch construction succeed; accepted `self.instances` is untouched. Later compile, abort, and commit ownership paths are unchanged in this diff. The 65-row late material-key miss test stages 64 earlier changes, observes the fallback, compares an independently replayed host with a cold compiler, then aborts, retries, and commits.
- Sparse intervals still admit via the existing cursor and coalesced dirty-slot ranges; the second sparse state derivation remains. They do not enter `is_exact_shape` or a complete-root updater. The new helper removes no sparse bound check, and the earlier duplicate mapping lookup and introduced double comparison are absent from the final complete callback. The work counters explicitly distinguish complete one-derivation-per-row from sparse two-derivation-per-dirty-row.
- `retained_rope.rs::is_exact_shape` checks the exact builder's 32-row leaves, final partial leaf, and recursive midpoint split. `ordered_plan.rs` calls it only after a successful, nonempty, complete admission with every physical row dirty. Thus mixed/all-Keep and sparse paths do not pay for the shape walk. A non-exact root is rebuilt from provisional states, not rederived input; the old, provisional, and compact roots can coexist temporarily. A separate skewed-tree test rejects full leaves in the wrong midpoint tree, and the 65-row fragmented all-dirty test checks exact pending shape, one derivation per row, and cold host output.
