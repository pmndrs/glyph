# Dense ordered-updater tradeoff advice — read-only review

**Decision:** No source change is supported yet. The fused complete admission removes the prior second `instance_state` derivation, but its remaining updater and exact-shape work are linear costs whose elapsed contribution is unmeasured. Fresh prior698 timing is pending. Current same-machine main/release results show dense512/1024 still slower, while inspection and sparse gains remain; they do not isolate this updater.

## Source-grounded work

In `ordered_plan.rs::prepare_retained_topology`, complete physical scope calls `pending_instances.update_ordered(0..count)`. Each old row reaches `retained_physical_index` (batch/key/slot and physical-order admission), `compare_retained_instance` (identity, binding dirt, one `instance_state`, equality), and, on Replace, `try_reserve(1)` plus dirty-slot push. The generic rope walker checks range advancement at each branch and row, reconstructs one final Vec for each changed leaf, recomputes the leaf summary, and builds changed branch shells. Keep leaves retain their Arc. The changed-leaf Vec is final storage, not an additional throwaway leaf allocation.

On nonempty all-dirty complete scope, `retained_instances.is_exact_shape()` separately walks the old tree to prove the exact 32-record leaf partition and recursive midpoint split. If false, `try_from_exact` copies already-computed provisional states into a compact root; it does not rederive them. Fragmented all-dirty can therefore carry old, provisional and compact roots temporarily. Mixed/all-Keep skip this shape walk and repack. The Inter1024 native receipt records 10,240 admission/comparison/derivation/rewrite visits per publication cycle, 13 shape nodes and one repack on initial fragmented publication, then 639 shape nodes and zero repacks on the next exact-shaped cycle. The benchmark fixture persists through sampling, so the initial repack is not charged to every timed sample. These counters do not count allocator calls or compiled instruction cost.

Prior698 admitted every complete old row first, then used `try_from_exact` for all-dirty output; that exact builder derived every row a second time but had a simpler direct leaf-fill loop. The present path removes the second derivation and avoids overwritten old-row copies, while adding a generic range walk and the conditional exact-shape proof. Both are O(G+K) on complete scope, with K actual leaves/nodes; exact packing still costs O(G) records and O(G/32) leaves. A simple latency cause cannot be inferred from the work-count delta.

## Why a replacement is not yet coherent

A direct exact builder cannot know that every compared row will Replace until it has admitted the final row. Building optimistically then encountering a late Keep or physical mapping miss can discard almost the entire tree and require fallback work. Staging all candidate states in a new flat vector would add another G-record allocation and copy; using that vector as retained storage would change the layout. An upstream semantic mask does not prove physical equality. Thus deleting the general updater for complete scope needs either repeated admission, speculative waste, or another storage owner. Mixed/all-Keep and sparse need Arc sharing; Stop, callback/storage errors and a late miss need the old accepted root intact and local dirty/binding scratch reset before the existing full-topology fallback. The current `prepare_internal` reset provides that boundary. A new complete executor or persistent exactness cache has no evidence gate.

Two bounded code questions could be checked only after fresh prior timing and exact optimized-Wasm attribution:

1. `OrderedRanges::advance_to` is called on every complete row even though its one interval is `0..count`. A change inside the existing walker could avoid repeated advancement while the current interval covers the row, retaining advancement at sparse range boundaries and Stop/error validation. Deletion target: repeated range-check work, not the updater. Falsifier: optimized Wasm already inlines/hoists the check, or sparse/size/p95 worsens.
2. The successful all-dirty exact root receives a separate `is_exact_shape` traversal (639 node visits in the exact Inter1024 cycle). Folding an exact-shape witness into the complete updater traversal might delete that walk, but it adds per-node proof work and must not burden mixed/sparse paths or mistake full leaves for the exact midpoint tree. Falsifier: added branch/code size exceeds the removed traversal, fragmented repack or peak memory worsens, or late-miss/error proof fails. This is a small O(K) coefficient target, not a likely explanation for the dense gap.

The per-Replace `try_reserve(1)` is also repeated, but scratch capacity is retained after warmup and `Vec::push` still needs capacity protection. Pre-reserving G would make all-Keep/late-miss cases pay memory they do not need. Any capacity-edge fast path needs compiled-cost evidence and the same fallible allocation behavior.

## Admission gates for any follow-up

Compare full physical bytes, ordered IDs/batch keys/bindings/draws and prepared reads to an independent cold compiler/host, including packed and fragmented roots, mixed/all-Keep, sparse dirty islands, Stop, late mapping miss, allocation/callback error, abort, rejection, retry and subsequent commit. Keep exact dense leaf partition/branch shape and old/in-flight root lifetimes. Count per-row/per-node visits, copied old rows, leaf/node allocations, repacks and peak live storage; inspect final optimized Wasm and raw/gzip delivery. Then use one authenticated complete-operation timing boundary against same-machine main, release and prior698 with unchanged sparse/inspection controls. No parity or causal latency claim follows from this note.
