# Portable E5 evidence packet

This packet preserves the completed E5 direct-hybrid measured wave as evidence, whether or not it is adopted. It contains exact byte copies of the recorded summaries, source patch and preservation manifests, full 145-identity workload table/CSV/quantiles, three original Labs report pairs, and the single profile's validated ancestry summary. Raw archives, raw matrix sample arrays, and raw CPU profile are deliberately excluded and inventoried by path, size, and SHA-256 in `copy-provenance.json`.

The current candidate package identity is SHA-256 `83949860ae65b99fe6d7471b9782398234852668b54412870fafa81878c366a3`. The preserved corrected-source patch is based on `277417596afbe2a8cd87454317d05607dc9ba51b` and describes source snapshot `be6c7960dd7eda8917bc43422edadca1970c1278`; those source identities are distinct from the packed package identity.

Observed matrix reports show the current candidate slower than prior E2 on the dense cases: `rewrite 512 labels in one group` reports +5.8%, p < .001, CI +4.9..+7.5%; `publish 1024 retained Text instances` reports +10.1%, p < .001, CI +8.5..+11.3%. These are the original Labs block-statistics results. Stored-sample nearest-rank p50/p95 values are separately labeled in the CSV/JSON and are not averaged across arms. This packet preserves the measured wave; it makes no adoption or promotion decision.

The main arm has three timing-mode exclusions and a 10.5% baseline software-calibration drift warning. Published has five timing-mode exclusions, 143 baseline identities versus 145 candidate identities (two candidate-only), and a 5.3% baseline software-calibration drift warning. Prior E2 has no timing-mode exclusions or calibration warning. The calibration labels are software estimates, not hardware-frequency measurements. Full exact exclusions, rows, original reports, and warnings are retained in `matrix/`.

The profile is a separate diagnostic, not a paired matrix timing. It used 1,024 setters, 10 warmups, 80 timed callbacks, no timed reads, and one scene update per callback. Its final topology was one draw with 9,216 glyphs. Fresh V8 ancestry assigned 790,207 µs of sampled CPU to the setter-descendant tree, 454,502 µs to the scene-update descendant tree, and 112,291 µs to unattributed stacks. The profiled callback's elapsed time is perturbed and should not be compared with the unprofiled matrix. The summary does not establish GPU time, exact phase latency, allocation bytes, or complete GC cost.

Source preservation and review files are in `source/` and `reviews/`. `architecture-speed-zoomout-sol-high.md` is intentionally pending and not copied because it is still being written. The reports here preserve scoped review conclusions; they do not substitute for a final product decision.
