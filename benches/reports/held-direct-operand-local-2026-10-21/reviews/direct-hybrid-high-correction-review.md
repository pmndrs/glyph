## Initial finding closed

`CodecGatherWorkspace::begin_direct` now calls `dirty_mask_ranges.try_reserve(record_capacity)` after clearing the candidate and before any row write (`codec_gather.rs:701–714`). A selected row can open at most one output interval; a bounds comparison and nonzero mask on the same row coalesce through `record_changed_output`. The candidate requires the old selected count and returns a miss on a selected-count/topology change, so reserving that count covers every admitted direct push. Reservation failure returns `AllocationFailed` before candidate row mutation; the predecessor workspace remains untouched. The first wave gains up to 9,216 `Range<usize>` payload slots, or 147,456 bytes on the native 64-bit fixture, with allocator capacity and Wasm size still to measure. It is not a memory-saving result.

The new `direct_zero_mask_translated_bounds_reserve_exact_output_intervals` test (`state.rs:13318`) uses a real Inter line-height change: the positioned producer reports `None` for full source coverage and zero semantic masks; direct admission visits five rows; the candidate emits exactly `[0..5]`; prepared and committed gather agree with a fresh packed gather, and committed physical bytes match an independent cold engine. The cfg(test) observer sits at the actual new-interval push (`codec_gather.rs:2222–2233`) and counts zero capacity-exhausted direct pushes. This targets the original failure mechanism rather than a fabricated internal state.
