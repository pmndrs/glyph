- `codec.rs:1201-1250,1342-1445`: the existing scalar and SIMD interpreter remains singular. `direct_view` is local to one scalar record; `direct_views` is local to one four-lane SIMD group. Each is initialized only at the first operation that survives `operation_buffer_masks & active_buffers`; later loads borrow the stored view. `DirectRowView` is not `Copy` or `Clone`, and no view arrays are reconstructed per later operation. Existing row resolution still occurs before the operation loop, so this patch does not change that E5 timing. The packed `v128_load` branch, active-operation order, lane 0..3 source calls, scalar tail, finite-output check, and store path are unchanged. SIMD does copy its small `DirectRowContext` array when entering a direct load branch, as E5 already did; it does not copy the 17-reference views.

It is evidence of constructor invocation count, not machine instructions, cache misses, latency, or Wasm SIMD speed. No public ABI or persistent engine state changed. Buckets 3 and 5 are explicitly the forward and reverse publications (`bitmap_replay_auth.rs:617-651`); the assertions do not impose a general production input restriction.

The new view's construction cannot produce a `GatherError`; actual field lookups still happen at the same active load through the existing resolver.
